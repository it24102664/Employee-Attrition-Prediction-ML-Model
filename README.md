# Employee Attrition Prediction using Machine Learning  

## 📌 Project Overview (Detailed)

Employee attrition, or employee turnover, refers to the phenomenon where employees leave an organization voluntarily or involuntarily. High attrition rates can cause significant costs for companies due to hiring, onboarding, and training new staff. At the same time, attrition is often linked to job satisfaction, compensation, work-life balance, and organizational culture.  

In this project, we aim to build a **Machine Learning–based predictive model** that can identify employees who are at high risk of leaving the company. By analyzing a combination of **demographic data, job-related attributes, and organizational factors**, the model will help HR teams and decision-makers understand **why employees leave** and **how to retain them**.  

### ✅ Objectives
- Perform **comprehensive preprocessing** of employee data, including missing value handling, categorical encoding, outlier detection, feature scaling, and feature engineering.  
- Conduct **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to uncover patterns such as attrition trends by gender, age group, income, job role, or promotion history.  
- Apply **Feature Selection** techniques (Mutual Information, Chi-Square, ANOVA, Random Forest Importance) to identify the most important predictors of attrition.  
- Apply **Dimensionality Reduction** using PCA to simplify the dataset while retaining most of its variance, improving training efficiency.  
- Build and evaluate supervised ML models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, etc.) to **predict attrition**.  
- Present results in a clear, interpretable way for non-technical stakeholders (HR managers).  

### 📊 Dataset Description
- **Dataset size**: 74,498 employee records  
- **Features**: ~24 attributes, including both categorical and numerical variables  
  - Examples: Age, Gender, Job Role, Monthly Income, Years at Company, Work-Life Balance, Job Satisfaction, Promotions, Dependents, Remote Work, Employee Recognition, etc.  
- **Target Variable**: `Attrition` → Stayed / Left  

### 🌟 Importance of the Project
- **Academic Value**: Helps students understand end-to-end ML pipeline, from raw data to predictive modeling.  
- **Business Value**: Supports HR in reducing turnover costs, improving employee satisfaction, and identifying key retention factors.  
- **Practical Value**: Builds teamwork by assigning each preprocessing step to a different member, reflecting real-world ML project collaboration.
